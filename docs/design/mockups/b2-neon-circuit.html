Neon Circuit

Cyber/tech HUD: two neon comets (cyan + magenta) chase around the edge, a scan band sweeps the scrim, line boxes are bottom-lit scan strips that wipe in.
  • Tap bubble - capture & recognise
  • Tap a line - copy whole line
  • Long-press a line - partial select, drag handles
  • X or Esc (system back) - close
  • Drag bubble to the bottom target - stop service
  • Tapping empty space does not close
